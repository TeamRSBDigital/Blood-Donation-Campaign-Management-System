# 🤝 Volunteer Manual - Pangsha Blood Donors Association (PBDA)

Welcome to the **PBDA Volunteer Field & Operations Guide**. As a PBDA volunteer, you are on the frontlines of collecting donor registrations and facilitating life-saving blood donations in Pangsha and surrounding Upazilas.

---

## 1. 📲 Field Registration of New Blood Donors
When registering a new blood donor during field campaigns or via telephone:
1. Open the PBDA app and click **"রক্তদাতা হন"** (Become a Donor).
2. Collect accurate details:
   * **Full Name** (পূর্ণ নাম)
   * **Mobile Number** (মোবাইল নম্বর)
   * **Blood Group** (রক্তের গ্রুপ: A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-)
   * **Location**: Division -> District -> Upazila -> Union.
   * **Last Donation Date** (সর্বশেষ রক্তদানের তারিখ) - System automatically calculates eligibility!
   * **Weight & Age** (ওজন ও বয়স)
3. Submit registration. Tell the donor they will receive verification once reviewed.

---

## 2. 🔍 Emergency Donor Search for Patients
When a patient family contacts you for urgent blood:
1. Go to **"রক্তদাতা খুঁজুন"** (Search Donors).
2. Select requested **Blood Group** and **Upazila** (e.g. Pangsha / Kalukhali / Baliakandi).
3. Ensure filter is set to **"শুধুমাত্র প্রস্তুত রক্তদাতা"** (Available Donors Only).
4. Call available donors directly using the phone action button.
5. Remind donors to bring a national ID and confirm they are feeling healthy before traveling to the hospital.

---

## 3. 📝 Submitting Emergency Blood Requests
If a patient family cannot find a donor immediately:
1. Click **"জরুরী রক্ত প্রার্থনা"** (Request Blood).
2. Fill in:
   * Patient Name & Hospital Name (হাসপাতালের নাম)
   * Blood Group & Number of Bags (রক্তের গ্রুপ ও ব্যাগ সংখ্যা)
   * Contact Mobile Number (যোগাযোগের নম্বর)
   * Urgency Priority (`CRITICAL` / `URGENT` / `NORMAL`)
3. The request will automatically ping PBDA admins and publish to the PBDA Telegram volunteer group.

---

## 4. 📞 Ethics & Conduct Guidelines
* Always treat donors and patient relatives with empathy, respect, and confidentiality.
* **Strict Zero-Fee Policy**: PBDA services and blood donations are 100% voluntary and free of cost. Never accept or request money.
* Verify donor health and ensure a minimum 90-day gap between blood donations for the donor's personal safety.
