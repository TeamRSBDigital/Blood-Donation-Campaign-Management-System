# 🤝 Volunteer Field Guide - PBDA System

## 1. Role Purpose
Volunteers (`VOLUNTEER` role) form the frontline operations team for PBDA, registering donors during field campaigns and connecting emergency blood requestors with available local donors.

---

## 2. Daily Field Workflow

### 2.1 Registering Donors in the Field
1. Log in to the Volunteer Portal using your provided volunteer account credentials.
2. Click **"নতুন ডোনার এনট্রি"** (New Donor Entry).
3. Record required fields:
   * **Full Name** (Bangla or English)
   * **Mobile Number** (Verify by calling on the spot if possible)
   * **Blood Group**
   * **Address**: Division -> District -> Upazila -> Union
   * **Last Donation Date**: If the donor has donated previously, select the date. The system auto-calculates if 90 days have elapsed.
4. Inform the donor that they are registered and will be notified during emergency calls in their area.

---

### 2.2 Assisting Patients with Emergency Requests
When a patient family requests help finding blood:
1. Open **"জরুরী রক্ত সন্ধান"** (Emergency Search).
2. Filter by required **Blood Group** and **Upazila** (e.g., Pangsha / Kalukhali).
3. Ensure the filter **"শুধুমাত্র প্রস্তুত রয়েছে"** (Available Donors Only) is enabled.
4. Call available donors directly from the list.
5. Reassure the patient family and submit an emergency request ticket in the system so admins can monitor progress.

---

## 3. Volunteer Code of Conduct
* **100% Free Service**: All blood donations and PBDA services are entirely voluntary and free. Never ask for or accept money.
* **Donor Health First**: Never encourage a donor to donate if less than 90 days have passed since their last donation or if they feel unwell.
* **Respect Confidentiality**: Protect donor phone numbers and patient medical details.
